NAL Fan Account deletion and data requests

National Arena League App users may request deletion of their NAL Fan Account and associated personal information.

The NAL App is operated by MNTR Inc. in connection with the National Arena League.

Delete Your Account in the NAL App

Option 1 — Delete your account from the app

Sign in to your NAL Fan Account and open your account/profile area. Select DELETE MY ACCOUNT and follow the instructions provided in the app.

Request Account Deletion Without the App

Option 2 — Submit a deletion request by email

If you cannot access the NAL App, you may request deletion by contacting NAL App Support.

Send your request from the email address associated with your NAL Fan Account whenever possible and state that you are requesting deletion of your NAL Fan Account.

REQUEST ACCOUNT DELETION

What Information Is Deleted

Account deletion may result in deletion or de-identification of information associated with your NAL Fan Account, including applicable account information, preferences, and associated activity.

Depending on your use of NAL App features, associated information may include:

  • Account and profile information;
  • Account preferences;
  • Pick ’Em selections and related account records;
  • Fan Forum activity associated with the account;
  • Poll or interactive-feature activity;
  • Fan submissions associated with the account; and
  • Other account-related information.

Information That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained when reasonably necessary or permitted for legal, security, fraud-prevention, backup, dispute-resolution, recordkeeping, or other legitimate purposes.

Information that is retained will be handled in accordance with the NAL App Privacy Policy and applicable law.

Request Deletion of Specific Personal Information

You may also contact NAL App Support to request deletion of specific personal information associated with your account without requesting deletion of the entire account. Requests will be reviewed and processed as required by applicable law.

Identity Verification

We may need to verify that you are the account holder before completing a deletion request. This helps protect NAL Fan Accounts against unauthorized deletion.
